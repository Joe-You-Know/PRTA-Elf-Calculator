**Goal:** Replace v3.3's 18 arithmetic joins for `scored_0149` (inputs 3,12,17; target set 1389,1572,1864,3071,4671,1687392) with fewer operations, rather than improving only geometric site placement.

**Exact search class:** Preserve the six target values and use only the original intermediate *numeric values* plus, optionally, ONE newly computed positive helper value, made by one binary arithmetic operation between two of the 21 original values. All candidate operations are +,-,×, exact quotient/remainder-checked division; candidate helper results <=10^10. Each original-value node may be regenerated by ANY arithmetically exact pair of the 21 baseline values plus that helper. Enforce one chosen construction per selected value, closure of dependency sources, explicit acyclic producer-before-consumer ranks, and at most 17 total join nodes. Distinct physical *occurrence identities* remain in the v3.3 baseline; value-node collapsing is expressly part of this restricted resynthesis hypothesis, not a general law of PRTA.
