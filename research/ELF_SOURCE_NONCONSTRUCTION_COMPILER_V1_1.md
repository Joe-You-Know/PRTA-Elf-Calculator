**Method:** Replay the complete source instruction history and assign an identity to every result occurrence even if numerically equal; preserve each instruction's actual two source occurrence IDs. For each requested target, consider first, last and joint greedy choice of *real previously constructed target witnesses* and trace their exact source ancestries. An instruction outside the selected ancestor closure is eligible for withholding from a *new program*. All new candidates are independently numerically replayed under the original positive, exact division, register limit and target witness rules. If removal changes register contents used later, candidate is rejected; no numerical zero, unobserved input, or omitted operation is treated as an arithmetic source. Source history remains in audit rather than being retroactively declared absent.
