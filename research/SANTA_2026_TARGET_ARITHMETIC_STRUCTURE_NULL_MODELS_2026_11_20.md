Genuine exact geometric progression with constant integer target ratio was very rare: only **one** such target triple in all scored puzzles.

**Power null distinction:** 20/23,942 scored targets > 1,000,000 are exact perfect powers; independent uniform integer null with matched decimal-length bands predicts ~0.611 exact squares and at most **0.6425** expected perfect powers via conservative union upper bound across prime exponents. Cannot test global power enrichment with target reassignment because the same numbers are preserved globally. The stronger signal is incompatible with independent size-uniform integers, but not proof of DLP/ECC generator.

**Within-puzzle translation control:** Add independent randomly chosen 7-digit positive integer to every target in a puzzle, preserving all within-puzzle absolute differences. Mean big-GCD pairs 3.7 versus original 409 across 40 translations. Thus cluster proximity alone doesn't explain large shared-factor count (but translation changes ratio distances and is NOT a null for relative-closeness measure).

**Interpretation:** Data strongly support arithmetic straight-line-program reuse / mixed additive-multiplicative construction, with powers often in hidden intermediate steps, rather than a direct set of visible multiplicative orbits. There is no independently established specific ECC curve, DLP structure, scalar-to-public map, or organizer motivation. Competing DLP-inspired hypotheses require generator documents or predictive, blind out-of-sample tests and should not be assigned numerical probability from resemblance alone.
