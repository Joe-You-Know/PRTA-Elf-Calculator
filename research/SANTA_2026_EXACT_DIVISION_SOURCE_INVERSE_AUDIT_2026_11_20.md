**Hypothesis:** Exact division is not, by itself, evidence of finite-field modular inversion; test how often solver reverses witnessed multiplication histories versus uses exact quotient extraction from other source constructions.

**Exact source occurrence method:** At each instruction preserve distinct source event IDs across register overwrites, original two operand IDs, result, operator and full arithmetic constraints. For a nontrivial `a/b`, determine whether the *exact current divisor-producing occurrence* belongs to the numerator's multiplication-only source ancestry. No association by merely equal integer values and no assumption that `a/b` implies an earlier source-produced multiplication. The true condition is `a=bq+r, r=0`; no prime p or field inverse demanded.

**Results:** Official sample has **1000 divisions all self-divisions a/a=1**, no nontrivial multiplication reversals. Strong 134,002-instruction conventional solver: **3646 divisions**, of which **426** are self-divisions making 1; remaining **3220** produce nonunit quotients not previously live in a register. Strict witnessed multiplication source occurrence cancellation is **155/3220 = 4.81%** of substantive divisions; **3065/3220 = 95.19%** lack that specific ancestry witness, though may encode other algebraic cancellations, factor extraction or distributed identities. Division first produces 141 scored targets (including 3 self divisions). Numerator producers across all strong divisions: input 575, multiplication 1254, addition 1016, subtraction 729, division 72. Independent second interpreter replays 1000/1000 official scored programs for BOTH original sample and optimized submission and matches all counts.

**Live alternative denominator controls:** For each division sample up to eight other actual live register values `d` with `1<d<a`, excluding the chosen denominator, and separately other live values with bitlength within two of chosen denominator. Unrestricted live alternative register placements: **3678 / 23341 = 15.76%** exact. Bit-size-matched alternatives: **4405 / 14974 = 29.42%** exact. The actual denominator is exact in 3646/3646 by problem rule; alternative figures are conditional on this *solver-generated* register pool, count register occurrences rather than distinct values, and are NOT independent random-number nulls or evidence of secret generator intentions.

**Conclusion:** Strong positive evidence that exact division is exploited as a useful *new quotient constructor* and that a limited set precisely undo witnessed multiplication-source factors; strong negative evidence that most strong-solver exact division is *direct source-factored multiplication reversal*. The requirement does **not** establish modular inversion in any finite field, elliptic curve or DLP generation. Distinguish solver traces from private puzzle-generator code.

**Next test:** symbolic source-preserving polynomial/quotient normalization of the 3065 non-ancestor quotients to discover whether they come from additive or indirect multiplicative cancellation, with frozen transformation rules and independent replay, then compare against generator-family-specific held-out puzzle outcomes. Avoid imposing an ECC grammar or asserting modular inverse without field boundaries.
