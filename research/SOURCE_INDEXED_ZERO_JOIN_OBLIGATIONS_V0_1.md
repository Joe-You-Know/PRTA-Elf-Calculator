# Which joining obligations are determined by source-indexed constructive zero?

Date: 2026-11-19. Source-grounded derivation, conditional implications, unresolved boundaries.

Primary read: PCL Complete Proof v1.0 §§3,23–26,29,38,64; user-supplied constructive_zero_qr_formalization.pdf; PRTA v122 README from original ZIP. This note is a derivation of conditional consequences, not an assertion that arbitrary withheld joins prove nonexistence.

Let source identity σ, rule γ, obligation J and exact examination witness w be retained. A source-indexed constructive-zero report Z(σ,J,γ,w) is an existing report, never literal absence or a signed negative quantity. A merely nonexecuted event is not itself a certified absence.

**Obligations closed/determined:**

1. Declared contribution: under the witnessed rule, no contribution of the specified type was admitted from source σ.
2. Source conservation: originally witnessed occurrences remain assigned or explicitly retained as residuals, not erased by a zero projection.
3. Exclusion: a downstream join requiring that precise absent contribution cannot be completed using it. Exclusions transfer only along source-identified, authorized dependency edges.
4. Residual ownership: unmatched positive occurrences and open face-receiving contacts remain source-owned until separately witnessed completion.
5. Typed closure: exact remainder closure is distinct from no-complete-group zero and from boundary/source closure; closing one channel does not erase another unresolved channel.

**Positive divisor**: For completed extraction D(X,B;γ) and positive B, counts N=|X|, P=|B|, Q=|complete groups|, R=|retained remainder| satisfy N=PQ+R and 0<=R<P. With a witnessed exhaustive no-group report Q=0_group, necessarily R=N and N<P. This does *not* follow from simply withholding an otherwise possible extraction. If R=0_rem from exact extraction, every original dividend occurrence belongs to one source-disjoint P-group, hence N=PQ; it does not establish that no grouping was performed.

**Zero divisor template**: With no positive B contribution the completed ratio grammar cannot execute; PCL §38 yields a failed/no-template attempt record, not a uniquely determined numerical Q or normalized R. No ordinary /0 instruction is legal in the Kaggle compiler.

**PRTA contact consequence**: The v122 source-anchored eight-by-four diagram contains 32 occupied UP, seven authorized DOWN and 14 withheld DOWN faces. Each separately authorized DOWN face fulfills its three specific receiving-edge obligations: 21 fulfilled and 42 unfulfilled interior contacts remain for the 14 withheld faces. Their absent/withheld state does not imply a missing numerical result, a negative count, or a physical ECC attachment. Without separately witnessed matching contacts, the 42 original receiving obligations are still open. Other joining consequences can be derived if the grammar states that the relevant port has exactly one lawful source and its status is exhaustive; otherwise other completions remain unknown.

**Bounded joining propagation proposal**: State S=(E,Z,U,O,G): witnessed constructions E, source-typed zero reports Z, unresolved candidates U, outstanding original obligations O, authored grammar G. Propagate only monotone, witness-backed implications: (a) no-group QR -> residual=N; (b) exact-rem -> remainder source obligation closed; (c) unavailable authorized contact -> original receiver unfulfilled, unless an independently admitted alternative contact provides its closure; (d) unique remaining producer after exhaustive candidate exclusions -> forced *planning obligation*, not an executed numerical result. Preserve proof dependency edges and the reason for each exclusion. Stop at a finite fixpoint, then compile only actual chosen arithmetic joins and validate all instructions.

**Important scope**: A source-typed zero alone does not determine all other obligations, every integer quotient, or absent physical faces. The compiler's candidate universe must be demonstrably complete for any forced-choice argument. Computational savings remain unmeasured.
