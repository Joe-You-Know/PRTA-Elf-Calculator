## Conditional sufficient law

v1.4 alpha-isomorphism preserved initial source slots, ordered operand-role source terms and the multiset of constructed occurrences but had no proof for physical contacts, time, register binding or source-owned omissions. v1.5 defines a conservative future boundary consisting of:

- whole source-owned construction term multiset, preserving multiplicity even when values coincide;
- source term to *actual triangular face* assignment, verified by finite patch three-edge incidence;
- register index to live source term binding, free-register capacity and source-specific remaining lifetime;
- contact receipts: producer term, operand role, actual source term, physical face path, shared vertex-pair edges, contact epoch and state (fulfilled, withheld, unresolved);
- separately witnessed withheld *actions* (source, obligation, witness), never equated to binary zero or literal source nonexistence;
- current epoch, patch radius, contact hop bound and declared grammar identity.

The key authorizes merging only when the complete source and boundary data match. A valid occurrence-preserving bijection maps all legal next actions in the **declared experimental physical contact machine** to corresponding next actions; if the transition rule depends only on that complete boundary this matching extends to finite futures by induction. The law is sufficient but not necessary, and is **not a universal PRTA theorem**. It deliberately rejects merges when any source-owned contact, register, placement, lifetime, withholding or examination rule differs. Physical paths are validated against actual triangular shared edges with source endpoints, occupied-relay guards and simultaneous receiver-port/relay collision guards.

## Independent evidence

Run `py -m unittest -q test_future_equivalence_v14 test_future_equivalence_v15` and `py audit_future_equivalence_v15.py`. **23/23 tests pass** (8 inherited, 15 new). Starting with numerical source occurrences (2,3), exhaustive two-join positive-integer histories with results <=50: **278 histories, 223 source-structural classes, 55 matching full-boundary chronological merges**. Independent exhaustive physically routed one-step frontier agrees for **55/55** candidate merges under a common authored placement/register/lifetime boundary. Changing a source lifetime blocks all 55 certificates; changing valid face placement produces a genuinely different next-contact frontier in an adversarial test. Forged source edge, wrong source-face attachment and duplicate receiver port are rejected. A witnessed withheld observation is not silently merged with no such witness. The census assigns a compatible *common posthistory boundary* and does not demonstrate that every historic contact path was actually reachable from its construction time.
