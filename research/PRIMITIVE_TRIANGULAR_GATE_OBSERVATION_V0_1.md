# PRTA primitive three-role gate observation v0.1 (2026-11-19)

User correction: their primitive is one triangular joining relationship, not a Euclidean square/orthogonal bit grid. The comparative geometric claim is that two equilateral triangular primitives compose one 60°–120° rhombus. This geometric area count is not itself a 2× silicon storage/energy demonstration.

**Implementation:** `prta_discrete_gate_observation_v0_1.zip` attached in conversation; source `primitive_gate.py`, exhaustive `test_primitive.py`, `benchmark.py`, audit JSON and README. Derived from user-supplied `weighted_nand_cell_model.pdf` and `absence_red_blue_v6.zip`, with explicit source/event distinction.

A gate has source roles A, B and receiving consequence C. The Boolean gate truth is retained. A causal observer/controller reads A and, if A determines C, **purposefully does not construct B's observation path**; B's underlying source may still exist but is not read, represented by a 0 or 1 value, or proven nonexistent by its lack of observation. This is source-specific nonconstruction of a *sensor request*, not literal absence of B, not a constructed zero, and not a transistor implementation. AND and NAND skip B when witnessed A=0; OR skips B when witnessed A=1. In all other cases B is observed.

**Exact exhaustive tests:** 7/7 unit tests pass. AND, NAND, OR each 4 possible binary assignments: 8 eager source observations vs 6 causal observations and 2 deliberately omitted B observations, 0 result mismatches. Compound AND(A,OR(B,C)) over all 8 assignments: 24 eager vs 14 causal source observations, 6 omitted observation-path events, 0 output mismatches. Two numerically equal bit sources retain separate occurrence identifiers.

**Limits:** Simulated observation-request counts only. No transistor area, electrical switching, speed or energy improvement established. This is ordinary short-circuit sufficiency represented as a discrete typed causal event ledger, not proof that the physical absence of an observation autonomously causes a new result. The next necessary experiment must distinguish input presence, actual sensor requests, causal contact closure, output transitions, and independent observation; compare circuits with identical Boolean behavior and device-level implementation before drawing hardware claims.
