Implemented reproducible `feedback_compiler.py` with recursive whole-source-network construction, forced alternative producer substitution, complete dependency closure, independent 48-register compilation, and a per-attempt physical contact check using v0.5 occurrence-indexed simultaneous contact routes and relay lifetimes. A physical failure is recorded as an **unresolved placement under the bounded authored contact grammar**, and triggers examination of the next full numerical producer family. Neither withheld alternatives nor failed contacts are translated into numerical zero/negative or claimed to be literally nonexistent.
