# PRTA Open-Plan Provisional Discharge Counterexample (2026-11-19)

**Status: Executed and independently checked bounded experiment, 4/4 new tests.** Source bundle in conversation: `prta_open_plan_provisional_discharge_probe.zip` containing actual exact v2.3 inherited event source and `open_plan_discharge_experiment.py`. Run the experiment with `py open_plan_discharge_experiment.py` in Windows.

Motivation: v2.3's proposed open-plan obligation boundary was treated as a requirement not to test early relay discharge. User correctly challenged this. We ran two physical scheduling policies without presuming either universal law.

**Grounded experiment** (v2.0 machine, radius-3 actual triangular face incidence, hop cap 4, initial distinct sources i0=2 face 2 and i1=3 face 4, start regs 0/1): first source-owned join j0=i0+i0=4 on face 0 with two distinct routed contacts and valid receipts.

- **Conservative:** declare two future duties `j0|A|j1|left`, `j0|B|j1|left` with `use_in_join` requiring exact future j1. Discharge is refused while pending. Exhaustively inspect available receiving faces for j1=j0+i1=7: **0** physically routable within declared patch/hop bound.
- **Provisional:** do NOT declare unknown future consumer; discharge both j0 receiving contacts with two immutable archived certificates, then attempt j1=j0+i1=7. **Physically routed at face 9**, with actual source j0 and original archive preserved. `verify_replay(seed,completed)` succeeds for v2.0. The v2.0 source identity and original exact receipts are not lost.
- **Closed-plan verification:** derive exact 2-join plan in v2.1, including both consumer duties on j0; pass provisional history to `verify_execution`. **Rejected** because the forward history did not declare the future duties. This must not be disguised as a proof of legal discharge under the v2.1 closed-plan consumer law. Rather it is a counterexample to the assumption that active relay persistence is the only way to realize a future *source occurrence* use; it is valid in v2.0 event grammar but rejected by the stronger v2.1 specification.

Four tests passed: guarded blocking, provisional future route, independently replayed exact source use and archive preservation, and explicit closed-plan rejection. The test establishes a meaningful opportunity for a new grammar in which a later consumer of a **constructed source occurrence** may be supported by its archived original contacts, provided the exact original source occurrence remains live, its historical joining receipts independently replay, and the later join is itself fully witnessed. Such a grammar must distinguish a future obligation still pending from its *support evidence already discharged*; it cannot just re-label pending as completed. This is a hypothesis requiring implementation and adversarial verification before full search. No Kaggle score change or physical-universality claim.

Correction: full open-plan obligation completeness was previously treated as prerequisite to even *test* physical effects of early discharge. That was unnecessarily restrictive. Next test: source-owned archived-support duty semantics with explicit outstanding future uses, lawful source release rules and final-time verification; compare against guarded policy under equal face/route/candidate budgets.
