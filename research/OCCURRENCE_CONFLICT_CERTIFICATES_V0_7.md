## Exact, scoped physical conflict

An obligation records (event ID, operand role, source occurrence, receiver occurrence, epoch). Under a complete fixed assignment to real triangular faces and a finite contact hop bound, the verifier enumerates real shared-edge paths and verifies simultaneous relay, receiver port, and live-face conflicts. An unsatisfiable obligation set is deletion-reduced to a core where removing any one retained obligation makes the remaining restricted constraints satisfiable. This is not a globally smallest cardinality certificate and does not prove universal physical impossibility. Overlapping face occupation has a separate typed error. Nonexhaustive alternative-placement searches remain unresolved.

## Coordinated repair

The repair search rebuilds complete arithmetic dependencies when changing source-certified producer families. It branches on events implicated by conflict cores, can try multiple authored placement options before changing producers, and can protect unaffected contacts by comparing exact (event,role,source,receiver,epoch,face path,actual edge witnesses) receipts. A construction failure never becomes numerical zero, an arithmetic operand, or proof of global nonexistence.
