## Measured algorithmic advance

Previously v2.2 was given a **closed** source-indexed arithmetic join graph, then jointly scheduled physical triangle faces, contact receipts, consumer duties, and discharge. v2.3 first **invents** bounded arithmetic candidate graphs from exact source-occurrence identities using +, positive -, multiplication and exact division. It retains repeated-source operand roles and complete source-join identity in its state key. It scores candidates against the entire target set through exact quotient/remainder. When all targets have been arithmetically constructed, its closed candidate is given to v2.2; the scheduler derives every mandatory direct future-consumer duty from that source graph **before** discharge, explores legal physical triangular face placement, actual edge paths and join order, then independently replays source receipts and emits 48-register legal arithmetic instructions. Failed finite physical routing is a **bounded unresolved result**, triggering consideration of other numerical graphs. A completed candidate is accepted only after independently verifying arithmetic and replay. Thus arithmetic graph generation and physical feasibility are *coupled through closed-candidate feedback*, NOT as a fully interleaved one-event-at-a-time search of incomplete graphs (where future obligations have yet to be discovered).

New tests independently discover and certify input (2,3): 2+2=4 repeated-source; 2+3=5 and 2*3=6 separate targets; and three-stage 2+2=4, 4+3=7, 7+2=9 with actual routed contact histories, closed consumers and certified relay discharges. All inherited tests pass.
